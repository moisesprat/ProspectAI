## Why

`config/tasks.yaml`'s `draft_strategy` STEP 3 currently asks the LLM to "decide" each stock's action by executing a near-complete decision table (branch on `entry_zone_status`, with hardcoded `momentum_score`/`financial_health` thresholds per risk profile, plus a "HARD STOPS" section that blocks the decision before any reasoning starts). This is precisely the kind of task LLMs are weak at (pattern-matching a memorized rule instead of genuine synthesis) — and it's the root cause already diagnosed in `critic-evidence-grounded-review`: the Critic inverted the CURRENT_ENTRY rule because it was asked to apply a table, not reason about one. It is also philosophically wrong for an agentic "Strategist": a real market analyst who sees strong technicals undermined by weak fundamentals doesn't consult a lookup table, they weigh the evidence and take a position with conviction.

The user explicitly requested this shift: let the Strategist (draft and final) and the Critic reason like analysts over empirical data, not execute matrices that code already does better — accepting more run-to-run variability in recommendations as a deliberate tradeoff, not a defect.

## What Changes

- Replace `draft_strategy` STEP 3's decision table with a reasoning framework (~250-350 words): present technical/fundamental/sentiment signals as inputs to weigh; instruct that conflicting signals have no pre-set resolution — the LLM must form its own conviction and name the tension explicitly in the rationale.
- Narrow the deterministic constraints to the two that are genuine logical/mechanical invariants, not risk-policy preferences: `entry_zone_status=CURRENT_ENTRY` excludes `WAIT-FOR-ENTRY` (semantic contradiction — "wait for entry" when already in the zone), and `price_data_error` caps the action at MONITOR/AVOID (no `current_price` means no valid `TradeSetup` can be constructed). Every other "HARD STOP" (`fundamental_unknown`, `financial_health=WEAK`, `composite_score<55`, sentiment-based blocks) becomes a factor the LLM must weigh and acknowledge, not a pre-reasoning block.
- **BREAKING**: `ActionPolicyGate`'s `ACTION_POLICY_TABLE` narrows accordingly — `BELOW_ZONE` no longer excludes `LONG-BUY` (only `WAIT-FOR-ENTRY` stays excluded there, since its "wait for price to fall into the zone" semantics don't apply below the zone). A Critic directive/critique ordering `LONG-BUY` at `BELOW_ZONE` is no longer dropped by the gate.
- Add a below-zone price-anchored trade-setup branch to `PortfolioAllocatorTool`, symmetric to the existing above-zone branch — required now that `LONG-BUY` at `BELOW_ZONE` is reachable, otherwise the entry zone/stop would sit above the current price while asserting an immediate buy.
- Trim the Critic's checklist to reasoning-coherence checks only. Remove entirely: `CONCENTRATION_BREACH`, `BUCKET_SUM_ERROR`, `MONITOR_WITH_SETUP`, `AVOID_WITH_SETUP`, `INVALID_ACTION`, `COMPOSITE_SCORE_MISMATCH`, `WAIT_ENTRY_ZERO_ALLOC`, `ALLOCATION_MISMATCH` — all already enforced deterministically by `PortfolioBoundsValidator`/schema validation/`ActionPolicyGate`, and the Final Strategist has no authority to fix a numeric field regardless (the Flow overwrites every numeric field unconditionally). Keep and loosen embedded numeric thresholds on the remaining reasoning-coherence checks (`ACTION_PROFILE_MISMATCH`, `UNCONVINCING_OVERRIDE`, `FABRICATED_SIGNAL`, `WEAK_FUNDAMENTALS_BUY`, `VALUATION_IGNORED`, `SENTIMENT_CONTRADICTION`, `VAGUE_RATIONALE`, `TRIGGER_CONTRADICTION`, `MISSING_STOP_LOGIC`) into qualitative language. Keep `PRICE_IN_ZONE_WAIT`/`WAIT_IN_ZONE` unchanged (the one true invariant).
- **BREAKING**: `CriticOutput.per_ticker_critiques` and `.revision_directives` relax from `min_length=1` to allow empty lists — a genuinely clean draft under the trimmed checklist can legitimately produce zero findings, which previously would have failed schema validation and forced fabrication.
- Reframe `final_strategy` STEP 1 from "apply the directives" to "weigh the Critic's counter-argument as a second analyst opinion and re-form your own conviction" — while keeping the existing hard requirement that every CRITICAL/MAJOR item is explicitly addressed (adopted or rebutted with cited evidence), so "weighing it" cannot degrade into silently ignoring it.
- Add a one-to-two-sentence identity principle to `investor_strategic` and `critic` backstories in `config/agents.yaml` ("reason like an analyst forming a conviction, not a rule-executor"); opportunistically remove stale `SCALED-ENTRY` references in `investor_strategic`'s backstory that already contradict the current schema.
- `investor_strategic`'s `temperature` stays at `0.1` (explicit user decision — variability comes from the prompt only, not from sampling).

Guardrails explicitly NOT loosened by this change: `PositionRecommendation.action` stays a closed `Literal["LONG-BUY", "WAIT-FOR-ENTRY", "MONITOR", "AVOID"]` (schema-enforced regardless of how the LLM reasons); `PortfolioAllocatorTool`'s pricing math, `PortfolioBoundsValidator`, `TradeSetup`/`InvestorStrategicOutput` invariants, `CompositeScoreTool`/`FundamentalGraderTool`/`TechnicalInterpretationTool`, the sentiment sentinel, the ETF filter, and Serper retry logic are all untouched.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
(none — this change modifies four existing capabilities)

### Modified Capabilities
- `reasoning-action-selection`: near-total rewrite. The fixed decision-table requirements are replaced with a reasoning-framework requirement (synthesize conflicting signals into a conviction-based action) plus the two surviving true invariants (`CURRENT_ENTRY` excludes `WAIT-FOR-ENTRY`; `price_data_error` caps at MONITOR/AVOID).
- `task-prompt-economy`: removes the "draft_strategy action-selection expressed as a decision table ≤20 lines" requirement (the fixed-table premise no longer applies) and replaces it with a word-budget requirement (~250-350 words) for the new reasoning-framework block.
- `action-policy-gate`: narrows the `BELOW_ZONE` policy-table entry to permit `LONG-BUY` (only `WAIT-FOR-ENTRY` stays excluded there).
- `reasoning-critic-validation`: removes requirements tied to checklist items now deleted (bucket/allocation/schema-adjacent checks) and to the deleted PULLBACK_ENTRY profile-bounds table column; loosens `ACTION_PROFILE_MISMATCH`/`UNCONVINCING_OVERRIDE` scenarios to qualitative thresholds; adds a requirement documenting that an empty `per_ticker_critiques`/`revision_directives` is a valid, non-fabricated outcome.
